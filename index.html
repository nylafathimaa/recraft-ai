      THE IDEA BEHIND RECRAFT AI

      Technology meets
      sustainability.

      A small idea with a bigger purpose: helping people see possibilities in the things they usually throw away.

      01

      What is ReCraft AI?

      ReCraft AI is an educational sustainability project that uses an image classification model to recognise three types of waste: plastic bottles, aluminium cans and cardboard boxes. After identification, it provides creative upcycling suggestions and step-by-step tutorials.

      02

      Why did we create it?

      Many everyday materials are thrown away even though they may still be useful. ReCraft AI encourages people to think differently about waste and explore ways to give materials another purpose.

      03

      Our sustainable goal

      Our project supports United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 12: Responsible Consumption and Production. We want to encourage thoughtful consumption, reuse and responsible choices.
