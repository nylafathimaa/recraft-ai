      GET TO KNOW US

      About ReCraft AI.

      Technology meets creativity for a more sustainable future.

      What is ReCraft AI?

      ReCraft AI is a sustainability-focused project that combines artificial intelligence with creativity to encourage responsible consumption and production. By using image recognition, the website helps users identify everyday waste materials and discover creative ways to give them a second life. Instead of immediately throwing items away, users can explore simple upcycling projects that turn waste into something useful. ReCraft AI aims to make sustainability more interactive, accessible and inspiring, showing how technology can help people make more environmentally conscious choices.

      🌍

      Our Purpose

      Encourage people to rethink waste and discover the value of reusing everyday materials.

      🤖

      How AI Helps

      Our image classifier recognises three waste categories and connects them to upcycling ideas.
